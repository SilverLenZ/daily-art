The practice

The pattern is visible. The message is structural.

Meet ZackThe person behind the patterns ↗

A line meets a circle. A rhythm resolves into a pattern. I follow these forms through sacred geometry, music, myth, and the natural world—different ways of approaching the same quiet mystery.

Some works are precise and diagrammatic; others open into color, symbol, and dream. Each one begins with something that catches my attention and asks to be followed farther. Trace the lines inward. Stay with what resonates.

Many pieces are available as high-quality prints, priced from $5 to $50. My wife, Maria Virginia, “SilverLOVECat,” creates original canvas paintings alongside this digital practice.
